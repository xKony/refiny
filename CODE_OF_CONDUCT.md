# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Refiny community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with people who are learning.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback about code, not people.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Acknowledging mistakes and focusing on what is best for the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive argumentation.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews — and when an individual represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that do not align with this code, and will communicate the reasons when appropriate.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@xKony](https://github.com/xKony), using the contact method listed on that profile. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be removed from the project team.
